##About Leonor Terible
Ms. #LEONOR TERIBLE was Human Resources Manager of Jolliville Group Management Inc. She graduated from the University of Santo Tomas, Cum Laude, with a degree of Bachelor of Science in Commerce, major in Business Administration. She has also some units for Masters in Business Administration from the University of the Philippines. She was a consistent honor student, graduating as Valedictorian in the Grade School, and Honorable Mention in High School.
As an employee, she has been a recipient of several Letters of Appreciation and Commendations from the highest officer of the command within her five years of stay in the U.S. Navy. She has been awarded Outstanding Employee during the Annual Performance Evaluation for the period January-December
1991. She was part of the Final Team in the U.S. Naval Base. Her last position then is Management Analyst at the U.S. Naval Supply Depot, Subic Bay.
Her expertise as Human Resources Manager includes Recruitment, Personnel Relations, Records Keeping and Training. As a Management Analyst, she specializes in Management Analysis, Project and Program Coordination, and Personnel and Administrative Work. Prior to her years with the U.S. Navy Command, she has been connected with different organizations/institutions like the National Economic and Development Authority (NEDA); banks including Associated Bank, the Far East Bank & Trust Company, and the American Express Bank; and private entities such as Syche Management Services, Inc. and the Aquino & Cudiamat, Inc. As a result of her employment with these organizations, she has gained varied work experiences, which include accounting, banking (particularly Branch Operations and Research Studies), and several administrative functions.
She was one of the Board of Directors of Syche Management Services as were as the Good Leaders Cooperation (UCMC). She is a Civil Service Eligible: PD 907 (granted for graduates with honors by the Philippine Government): Career Service Professional and Sub Professional. She received several trainings in Management Development.
